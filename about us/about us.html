About Us

We are the Artists 2026—a batch of 38 individuals from Albert Einstein School (AES), bound by creativity, ambition, and resilience. Our batch name, Artists 2026, was given to us by our school founder, Sir Edison B. Morales. Before we became the Artists, we were known as the Junior Visionaries, named after an earlier batch of AES, the Visionaries. As we grew, so did our identity—shaped by our experiences, challenges, and aspirations.

Being an Artist isn't just about talent—it's about vision. It's about seeing beyond what exists and imagining what could be. We see what others only catch a glimpse of. Whether in academics, the arts, leadership, or innovation, we push boundaries, challenge expectations, and strive for excellence in everything we do.
